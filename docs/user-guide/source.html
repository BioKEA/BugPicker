1. Purpose and Responsibilities

BugPicker scans sorting trays, identifies candidate specimens, picks with vacuum nozzle N1, places specimens into a 96-well plate, verifies nozzle and well images, and records images and metadata. Separate programs support hand-plated wells, taxonomy review, QA labeling, model evaluation, retraining, and candidate promotion.

Scope. Routine operators should not change machine limits, camera definitions, G-code, model architecture, or script source.

The operator must confirm tray and plate placement, watch initial motion, answer review windows accurately, preserve specimen identity, and stop whenever physical motion does not match the displayed plan.

2. Safety and Emergency Response

{{IMAGE:emergency-controls.jpg|Machine power and emergency controls.}}
Stop immediately if a nozzle approaches hardware at an unsafe height, a tray is not seated, coordinates are clearly wrong, or a cable can snag.
  1. Use the run Halt control when there is time to stop normally.
  2. Use machine power or emergency stop for an imminent collision. Do not rely on a dialog.
  3. After a crash, inspect N1, holders, cameras, plates, and loose specimens before motion.
  4. Restart and home only after removing the obstruction. Watch homing continuously.
Homing is not proof of clearance. Confirm N1 is at safe travel height before XY motion. Never leave the first movements unattended.

4. Starting OpenPnP

  • Clear the complete travel area.
  • Seat trays, plates, and holders.
  • Connect cameras and machine USB devices.
  • Start OpenPnP and enable power.
  • Home while watching the head and N1.
  • Verify Top, bottom, and HiRes live feeds.
  • Verify N1 can reach safe travel height.
{{IMAGE:openpnp-home.png|OpenPnP after successful startup and homing.}}

Resolve any gray, frozen, or red-X camera before beginning. A disconnected HiRes camera may require closing OpenPnP, reconnecting USB, and restarting.

5. Preparing a Plating Run

  1. Seat each sorting tray consistently; no edge should be raised.
  2. Spread specimens to reduce overlap.
  3. Seat destination and recovery plates in calibrated slots.
  4. Record collection code, plate number, starting well, tray assignments, and classifier modes.
  5. Open RUN_BUGPICKER.js.
{{IMAGE:tray-loaded.jpg|Correctly seated automation tray with separated specimens.}}

Run Layout

The setup window defines active collection events, trays, plate numbers, starting wells, debris/taxonomy modes, and auto re-plate. Tray-to-event assignments protect sample identity.

6. Calibration

Calibrate to seated hardware. Tray height and tilt can change with seating.

The window controls tray bounds/profile, plate positions, HiRes-over-A1 and focus, and recovery position. Use jog and Record controls instead of estimating coordinates.

{{IMAGE:calibration-window.png|Calibration window with tray, plate, HiRes A1, and recovery controls.}}
  1. Select the correct slot and tray profile; use Automation tray for rigid automation holders.
  2. Verify scan bounds with the Top camera.
  3. Center HiRes over A1 and confirm the focus sweep is collision-free.
  4. Verify Top-camera A1 and recovery A1 for automated plating.
  5. Raise N1 before accepting calibration.

Automation-tray depth depends on specimen size and can descend progressively on repicks, never below the physical floor. Repeated initial-height failures can trigger adaptive rescanning; no-progress and generation guards prevent endless loops.

7. Running BugPicker

  1. Watch N1 rise before scanning.
  2. Verify the first scan row is over the tray.
  3. Review targets; exclude debris, unsafe overlaps, and unpickable items.
  4. Watch the first complete pick and placement.
  5. Monitor camera feeds, nozzle QA, well QA, and recovery.
  6. Do not close OpenPnP while intentional child processes are active.
{{IMAGE:scan-review.png|Target review showing included and excluded targets.}}

Decision Sequence

Bottom inspection confirms material after a pick. If none is detected, the well remains unattempted. After placement, the Top whole-well image is evaluated first. Occupied wells receive a HiRes image; apparently empty wells can be retried after bottom inspection. Material remaining on N1 enters recovery/cleaning.

After three recovery attempts, the current workflow continues rather than ending solely on a likely nozzle false positive. Images remain available for labeling.

8. Reviewing and Completing a Plate

The summary distinguishes attempts, successful placements, second-attempt successes, and wells needing review. Treat the physical plate as authoritative.

{{IMAGE:plate-review.png|Completed-run plate review or filled plate map.}}
  1. Review wells flagged potentially empty and correct occupancy.
  2. Review the full plate map for unexpected gaps.
  3. Confirm images and rows correspond to physical wells.
  4. Continue auto re-plate only while valid specimens remain.
  5. Review the completed-plate audit before taxonomy.
H12 is the negative control and is not filled.

9. Hand Plating and Manual Imaging

Use RUN_MANUAL_PLATE_IMAGING.js for a plate filled wholly or partly by hand.

{{IMAGE:manual-imaging.png|Manual setup and HiRes-over-A1 calibration.}}
  1. Place the plate in the normal destination position.
  2. Enter plate number, collection code, and first/last occupied wells.
  3. For an existing BugPicker plate, keep Do not replace wells already in the spreadsheet selected. Recorded occupied wells are skipped, including around gaps.
  4. Move HiRes to A1, center/focus, and record the position.
  5. Start and watch the first movement.
This mode does not detect occupancy. Every spreadsheet-empty well in the selected range is assumed hand-filled. Exclude physically empty wells.

Normal plate images, CSV, and XLSX are updated with manual plating HiRes confirmation.

10. Taxonomy Workflow

Use RUN_TAXONOMY.js for a completed plate without running the picker.

{{IMAGE:taxonomy-review.png|Taxonomy review with identification, image consistency, and notes.}}
  1. Confirm all images belong to the same well/specimen.
  2. Review proposed taxonomy rather than accepting confidence alone.
  3. Record image consistency accurately. Notes other than “Same specimen across images” enter the plate Notes field.
  4. Complete/save review as directed.
  5. Verify taxonomy outputs and spreadsheet before archiving.

11. QA and Model Maintenance

Use the candidate workflow: label, evaluate, train, audit, then promote after reviewing the recommendation. Training does not replace the active model automatically.

{{IMAGE:qa-label-review.png|QA label review with available labels.}}{{IMAGE:model-report.png|Candidate report and promotion recommendation.}}
  1. Collect representative empty and occupied nozzle/well images.
  2. Run RUN_QA_LABEL_REVIEW.js; prioritize errors, low confidence, difficult lighting, and unreviewed images.
  3. Run RUN_QA_CV_EVALUATION.js for cross-validation without changing the active model.
  4. Run RUN_QA_MODEL_TRAIN.js to train and evaluate a candidate.
  5. Run RUN_QA_CANDIDATE_AUDIT.js for nozzle safety and empty-clearance gates.
  6. Promote only when supported by the report or a maintainer documents an override.
  7. Restart the relevant workflow after promotion so it reloads the model.

Dataset size alone is insufficient. Nozzle QA needs truly empty examples spanning runs, rotations, lighting, and nozzle appearances that resemble live failures.

12. Program Directory

Main scan, review, pick/place, QA, recovery, replate, summary, and audit workflow.

RUN_MANUAL_PLATE_IMAGING.js

HiRes images and records for hand-plated wells.

RUN_TAXONOMY.js

Taxonomy classification/review for an existing plate.

RUN_CAPTURE_EMPTY_NOZZLE_QA.js

Captures confirmed-empty N1 images with rotation variation.

RUN_CAPTURE_EMPTY_WELL_QA.js

Captures confirmed-empty whole-well Lumen images.

RUN_QA_LABEL_REVIEW.js

Labels nozzle/well QA images, including prioritized difficult samples.

RUN_QA_CV_EVALUATION.js

Cross-validates QA behavior without promotion.

RUN_QA_MODEL_TRAIN.js

Trains a QA candidate and reports a recommendation.

RUN_QA_CANDIDATE_AUDIT.js

Audits a nozzle candidate and offers promotion after passing.

RUN_MODEL_UPDATE.js

Updates debris/taxonomy datasets, trains candidates, and writes history.

RUN_REVIEW_MODEL_PROMOTION.js

Promotes selected completed candidates without retraining.

Do not launch numbered or lower-level Python scripts routinely unless instructed by a maintainer or recovery procedure.

14. Troubleshooting

SymptomResponse
HiRes gray, frozen, red-X, or absentHalt. Close OpenPnP, reconnect Teslong USB, restart, home, and verify capture.
invalid port descriptor: /dev/ttyACM0Restore/check the controller’s Linux device, then restart OpenPnP.
Soft-limit errorStop. Verify homing, offsets, selected slot, and calibration; do not simply raise limits.
N1 misses in X/YStop; recheck tray seating, scan calibration, transform, and first-target position.
N1 too high or compressing specimensVerify tray profile, seating/tilt, size class, and adaptive generation. Never exceed the physical floor.
Empty N1 reads occupiedPreserve images; label matching failures/empty captures, retrain, audit, then promote.
Training appears stuckCheck live log and CPU. If OpenPnP is closed, terminate only confirmed orphaned BugPicker workers.
Auto re-plate stopsCheck open wells, unattempted targets, no-progress guard, and generation limit.
Images/rows missingPreserve the scan and use audit/repair with a maintainer. Do not rename source files.
Script missingOpen BugPicker → 00_Run_BugPicker; refresh the menu or restart OpenPnP.
After a collision, coordinate anomaly, or unexpected Z movement, identify the cause before retrying.

15. Shutdown and Handoff

  • Confirm no run, taxonomy, update, or training process is active.
  • Park safely and remove specimens/plates.
  • Verify CSV/XLSX, images, audit, and taxonomy outputs.
  • Record unresolved failures or hardware concerns.
  • Close OpenPnP normally and power down per lab procedure.

16. Maintaining This Guide

Edit docs/user-guide/source.html, keep stable image filenames, and rebuild:

python3 docs/user-guide/build_user_guide.py

The builder ensures every current launcher is documented. Missing images become labeled placeholders. See docs/user-guide/assets/README.md.

  • Update affected procedures and program entries.
  • Replace screenshots when labels/layout change.
  • Update safety language when motion changes.
  • Build and resolve validation failures.
  • Review HTML and PDF before release.
